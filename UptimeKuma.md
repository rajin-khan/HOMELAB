# 📊 [Uptime Kuma](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma) & Why I Took It Out

> [Uptime Kuma](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma) checks whether your apps are responding and can let you know when they stop.

I took it out of the homelab because the constant emails got annoying.

---

## 🏡 What I Use on the Pi Now

- **When I want a quick look**, [Homepage](https://github.com/gethomepage/homepage) has the app shortcuts and USB drive status. [Homepage Guide](Homepage.md)
- **When I have a question**, I ask Aria what's running or where a request got stuck. I can also get storage alerts through Telegram. [Hermes Guide](Hermes.md)

---

## 💡 When a Status Page Still Makes Sense

If other people use your homelab, a status page can answer “is the media server down?” without giving anyone your admin login. If you're adding it to your setup, I'd start with one or two important checks and pick notifications you'll actually want to read. [Uptime Kuma project and setup](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma)
